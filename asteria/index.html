Cyprus Golden Visa

A home in Larnaca. A Golden Visa for your family.

For buyers from outside the EU, an Asteria residence can open the door to the Cyprus Golden Visa: permanent residency for the whole family.

€300,000

Golden Visa Threshold

Investing €300,000 or more, plus VAT, in a new home bought directly from a developer can qualify non EU buyers for Cyprus permanent residency. Eleven of the twelve Asteria residences are priced at or above this threshold on their own.

Family

One Permit, Whole Family

The permit covers your spouse and children under 18. Applicants show a secure annual income from €50,000, and visit Cyprus at least once every two years.

EU

A Base in the European Union

Cyprus has been a full EU member since 2004 and uses the euro: a stable Mediterranean base for your family, your investment and your future.

Golden Visa eligibility is assessed by the Cyprus Civil Registry and Migration Department and criteria may change. Buyers should take independent legal advice; the MAB Developments team can introduce you to specialist lawyers.

The story behind Asteria

Half Lebanese, half Cypriot. A homecoming, built from A to Z.

Since Mihran A. Beuyukian founded MAB in 1960, two generations of the family have shaped how the region works with wood, aluminium and fine finishes: equipping the workshops and industries that build high end interiors, and knowing every material, every joint and every coating from the inside.

Today the company is led by Raffi and Alex Beuyukian. Half Lebanese and half Cypriot, they had built a lifetime of expertise, yet had never invested in their own homeland. So they asked a simple question: why not here? Why not create a product from A to Z, on Cypriot soil, and put everything they know about materials and interiors into it?

Not another brick and mortar block that looks like every other building on the street, but a landmark that adds value to its neighbourhood and to the market. That is Asteria, the first project of MAB Developments.

Questions & answers

Everything buyers ask about Asteria.

Can't find your answer? Our team replies within 24 hours.

Can I get a Cyprus Golden Visa by buying at Asteria?

Non EU buyers who invest at least €300,000 plus VAT in a new home bought from a developer can apply for Cyprus permanent residency, known as the Golden Visa. Eleven of the twelve Asteria residences are priced at or above this threshold on their own. Final eligibility is decided by the Cyprus Civil Registry and Migration Department.

Where exactly is Asteria Residence?

Asteria is on Plot 132, Markou Drakou Street, Larnaca, Cyprus: across the street from The American Academy, a 5 minute drive from Finikoudes Beach and 15 minutes from Larnaca International Airport.

How many apartments are there, and what sizes?

Only 12 residences across 5 floors: one and two bedroom apartments from 51.6 m² to 100.2 m² internal, including three 4th floor penthouses with private roof terraces.

How much does an apartment at Asteria cost?

Prices range from €235,000 to €575,000 plus VAT. Current pricing and payment plans for each residence are available on request from the MAB Developments sales team.

Can I rent my apartment on Airbnb?

Yes. Owners can let their residence on short or long term contracts. Short term letting in Cyprus requires registration with the Deputy Ministry of Tourism.

When will Asteria be completed?

Construction started in September 2026, and completion in Q2 2028 is written into the sale contract.

Who is the developer behind Asteria?

Asteria is developed by MAB Developments, led by Raffi and Alex Beuyukian. Their family company, MAB, was founded in 1960 and has two generations of expertise in wood, aluminium and fine finishes.
